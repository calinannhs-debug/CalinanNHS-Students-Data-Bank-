Student Data Bank

Welcome to the Calinan National High School student information collection portal. This page provides a convenient way for authorized school personnel to collect student information through Microsoft Forms.

Important: This website is a collection portal only. Student responses are submitted to the Microsoft Form configured by the school; this static GitHub Pages website does not store student records itself.

Privacy Notice

Student information is confidential. Collect only information needed for an approved school purpose. Do not publish student names, addresses, contact details, learner reference numbers, or submitted responses on this public website or in a public GitHub repository.

  • Before collecting data, the school should provide its approved privacy notice and identify the responsible school office or data protection contact.
  • Configure Microsoft Forms so only the intended respondents can access it, where appropriate, and restrict response access to authorized staff.
  • Do not request passwords or unnecessary sensitive personal information in the form.
  • Use a school-managed Microsoft account, enable appropriate account security, and limit access to response spreadsheets.
  • Follow the school's records-retention and data-protection procedures. Obtain any required parent/guardian consent or authorization before collecting information from minors.

This template is not a substitute for the school's approved privacy notice or legal review.
